About UIU Bus Service

UIU-BUS is the official shuttle booking platform of United International University. Browse routes and live availability freely — a Student ID is only needed when you reserve your seat. Drivers sign in through a separate portal and share their live location so every trip stays safe and on schedule.

💺 Seat Booking

Pick any available seat across 6 campus routes. One Student ID = one seat per trip, enforced on the server.

🗺️ Live Location

Watch shuttle routes on the live map, get directions, and receive bus-near alerts on Telegram.

🔐 Secure Accounts

Separate Student and Driver logins with hashed passwords; drivers verify GPS before entering the dashboard.
